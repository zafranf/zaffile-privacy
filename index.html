Privacy Policy — Zaffile

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Zaffile - File Manager ("the app", package id.zaffweb.zaffile) is a file manager and gallery for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Files on your device

The app reads the photos, videos, audio files and documents you choose to browse, preview or edit. All processing — including crop, brush, rotate, trim, speed change and save — happens on your device. Edited files are written back to your own storage, in the same folder as the source file unless you choose otherwise. We do not upload, copy or back up your files to any server.

Permissions

  • Media access (photos, videos, audio): to show your media library and let you pick files.
  • Full file access (optional): only if you grant it, so the app can browse folders that are not indexed by the system media database.
  • Notifications (optional): to show progress for long operations such as copying many files.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general app interaction data, as described in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where required by law, a consent prompt is shown before ads are requested.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics of your file names, folder names or file contents.
  • No upload of your photos, videos or documents.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

The app itself keeps no server-side data. Settings and recent-folder counters are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
